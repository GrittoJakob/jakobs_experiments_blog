About

Hi, I'm Jakob. I am a student in Robotics, Cognition, and Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). My main interests are reinforcement learning and optimal control.

On this blog, I want to share some thoughts, experiments, and implementation details from my projects. I really enjoy reading research blogs, and when I started implementing MPO, I wished I had found a blog post about it. So I thought, why not write one myself? Hopefully, this can help other students too, if anyone ever finds this website :)
